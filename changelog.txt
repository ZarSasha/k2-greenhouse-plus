Version: 1.5.0
Date: 2026-09-25
  Balancing:
    - Created a new distillation recipe exclusive to the biochamber, for the sake of more accurate
      balancing that keeps the difference in yield between the carbonization/distillation process
      chains within a reasonable range even as bonuses to productivity increase. The new recipe
      produces a bit less coal and petroleum gas to counter the 50% productivity bonus. Assuming
      maximum use of biochambers, as well as the use of an electric boiler for steam, the petroleum
      gas yield ratio should be like this: Solid fuel starts at ~128.0% and rises towards ~138.0%,
      while petroleum gas start at ~119.5% and falls towards 106.5%. Good enough!
  Changes:
    - Reordered recipes.
    - Created new names for some recipes and settings. Created migrations for it.
  Info:
    - Fixed minor errors in changelog entry for v1.4.6.
    - Updated description of recipes and some related settings.
    - Added a Factoriopedia entry for the biochamber wood distillation recipe.
  Locales:
    - Updated Chinese translation with DeepL.
  Notes:
    - The Chinese translation relies a lot on DeepL now, and I cannot guarantee the quality of it.

Version: 1.4.6
Date: 2026-09-24
  Balancing:
    - Pyrolysis: Carefully rebalanced the distillation recipe so that it always maintains at least
      a small advantage over the carbonization recipe in regards to yields on petroleum gas, even
      as it tends to diminish with increasing productivity bonuses (due to differences in chain
      lengths). The distillation recipe is still usable in the biochamber, to keep it ahead.
      Assuming the use of an electric boiler for steam, the ratio for solid fuel was slightly
      increased to ~104.5%, rising towards ~116.0% (with biochambers: ~157.0%, rising towards
      ~165.5%), but for petroleum gas, it should be ~123.5%, falling towards ~103.0% (with
      biochambers: ~150.0%, falling towards 131.5%).
  Info:
    - Created a migration script informing the player of the change above.
  Notes:
    - Balancing for productivity and biochambers require a lot of compromise. I had to give up on
      energy parity between the pyrolysis recipes, and the disparity compounds with productivity.
      But at least the distillation recipe, which is more complicated, remains at an advantage, and
      that seems more important. (Of course, the investment cost already tends to be a lot lower,
      but that's a metric that probably doesn't matter to those who seek to grow the factory.)
    - I may create recipes that are unique to the biochambers and replaces distillation in the
      the future, for better balance and higher realism.

Version: 1.4.4
Date: 2026-09-23
  Balancing:
    - Advanced pyrolysis recipe: Very slightly increased the amount of petroleum gas produced and
      also allowed it to work in the biochamber from the Space Age DLC! This is meant to give the
      "advanced" oil chain an advantage over the "basic" one in the late-game, where extreme
      productivity bonuses tend to favor overall longer production chains in regards to yield.
      (There is no perfect way to balance this, the productivity mechanic is the problem).
    - Greenhouse for wood:
      - Once again made pollution absorption active (so it can also be read from the tooltip),
        just like how it is with the biochamber in the Space Age DLC. The problem of the game
        engine limitation is mitigated by the solution from v1.4.2.
      - Made pollution absorption scale with the base output rate.
  Info:
    - Pyrolysis recipes: Changed names to "Wood carbonization" and "Wood distillation" and added
      short descriptions. This was done to give less of an impression that the "advanced" recipe is
      always better than the "basic" one for the most important oil production (productivity
      bonuses from machines and legendary modules can flip things on their head down the line, at
      least if you only care about maximizing yields from an input; but that also changes again
      with the biochamber in Space Age, since the "advanced" recipe works in it now).
    - Updated description of setting related to the greenhouse for wood.
    - Fixed changelog entry for v1.0.0.
  Locales:
    - Updated Chinese translation with DeepL.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2025-02-15
  Changes:
    - Initial release.
    - Extracted code and assets for the greenhouse from "Krastorio 2" by raiguard and ported them
      to Factorio 2.0. Included the GNU LGPLv3 license, which applies to most of the assets.
    - Adapted mod to work with the Space Age DLC and various other mods.
    - Requires base >= 2.0.7 for automatic technology effect reset on configuration changed.
  Minor Features:
    - Created 3 greenhouse variants that produce either wood, yumako fruits or jellynuts. The first
      one absorbs pollution, but the rest release spores in proportion to their production speed.
    - Created pyrolysis recipes for creating coal and oil from wood:
      - Basic wood pyrolysis (furnace): 6 wood -> 3 coal.
      - Advanced wood pyrolysis (chemical plant): 15 wood -> 20 crude oil, 15 petroleum, 3 coal.
    - Created several startup settings:
      - Enable greenhouses for yumako fruits and jellynuts. Can be set to require overgrowth soil
        or just artificial soil for their recipes.
      - Enable early research of Coal Liquefaction tech.
      - Configure the amount of module slots available in all greenhouses.
      - Individually configure output rate for each of the 3 greenhouses.
    - Added circuit wire connections to greenhouses.
    - Compat for "AAI Industry", "Factorio+" and "Glass": Changes greenhouse recipe ingredients,
      replacing iron plates with some variant of glass.
  Balancing:
    - Greenhouse for wood may normally be unlocked after Steel Processing and Landfill tech; the
      other variants with the appropriate soil technology, depending on startup setting.
    - Tree seeding will be unlocked early, along with the greenhouse for wood. The relevant tech
      had its cost lowered to compensate (not removed, since it has dependents).
    - The basic pyrolysis recipe is available from the start, while the advanced one may be unlocked
      with Oil Processing (requires only Fluid Handing, has a fairly small research cost).
    - Coal Liquefaction can be researched right after Advanced Oil Processing, at much lower cost.
    - All recipes for crop growth are very simple. Just add water!
    - Carefully balanced both pyrolysis recipes to be useful, but for different production chains.
    - Modified recipe for greenhouses to require steel plates, iron/glass plates, circuits and the
      appropriate seeds + soil.
    - Greatly increased overall cost of greenhouses, mostly because it is a renewable source of
      energy. Still a lot cheaper than solar panels.
    - Greenhouse for wood by default outputs as much wood as 37.5 planted trees and outputs 0.25
      wood/s. In practice, this seems to net a power output pr. area at least twice that of solar +
      accu. But:
    - Greenhouse for wood absorbs only 1.125 pollution/min (as much as 37.5/2 trees), which is
      about 13.5% of what is emitted when burning its wood output in a Boiler, so it's not a clean
      energy source!
    - Balanced yumako and jellynut recipes so output with the legendary tier greenhouse is still a
      bit less pr. area than with the agricultural tower.
    - Requires certain surface conditions for each greenhouse. The one for wood can be built on
      Nauvis and Gleba (since wood can be acquired in both places), the rest only on Gleba.
    - Greenhouse module slot amount set to 0 by default, since planted trees and agricultural
      towers cannot receive such effects.
    - Halved the cost of wood for the tree seed recipe, feels nicer.
    - Note: Higher quality greenhouses work faster, without producing more spores. I cannot fix
      that with the current API.
  Graphics:
    - Created colored variants of the greenhouse for each type of crop.
    - Removed working animation flickering, barely noticable for the original, and too apparent
      with other colors. Greatly reduces file size with many color variants!
    - Created some simple, custom remnants for the greenhouses.
    - Created a custom explosion effect for the greenhouses.
    - Added impact category and impact effect for the greenhouses (related to sound as well.)
    - Increased size of greenhouse alt-view recipe icon.
    - Created circuit wire connection visuals for greenhouses.
    - Created a thumbnail with a proper preview of the greenhouse with shadows.
  Sounds:
    - Got rid of working sound effect (white noise).
  Info:
    - Created a README.md file explaining the mod.
